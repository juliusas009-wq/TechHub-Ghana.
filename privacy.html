1. Introduction

Welcome to TechHub Ghana. We provide technology news, programming tutorials, artificial intelligence features and digital tools for students and other technology users.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or use TechHub Ghana.

By using the website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with these practices, you may choose not to use the relevant features or the website.

2. Information We May Collect

The type of information involved depends on how you use TechHub Ghana. Information may include:

  • Information you provide: such as an email address if you voluntarily subscribe to a newsletter or contact us.
  • AI requests: text or other content that you voluntarily submit to the TechHub Ghana AI Assistant.
  • Technical information: information that may be automatically available to website infrastructure, hosting or security services, such as IP address, browser type, device information and request information.
  • Usage information: information about how visitors interact with the website when analytics or similar technologies are enabled.

TechHub Ghana does not intentionally ask users to provide unnecessary sensitive personal information.

3. AI Assistant and AI Services

TechHub Ghana provides AI-powered features that may help users rewrite, summarize, improve, explain or otherwise process text.

Important: Do not submit passwords, bank card information, banking credentials, government identification numbers, private account credentials or other highly sensitive information to the AI Assistant.

How AI requests are processed

When you submit a request to the TechHub Ghana AI Assistant, the information required to process that request may be sent through the TechHub Ghana AI backend to an AI processing service.

TechHub Ghana currently uses Cloudflare Workers AI for AI model processing through an AI binding configured on the TechHub Ghana AI Worker.

The AI Assistant should therefore be treated as an online processing service. Users should only submit information they are comfortable sending for processing.

TechHub Ghana does not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the AI Assistant.

AI Search

TechHub Ghana may also use AI Search or related search infrastructure to help users find or retrieve relevant website information.

Search requests may be processed by the services required to provide the search functionality. Users should avoid entering confidential information into search fields.

4. Newsletter Information

If you choose to subscribe to TechHub Ghana updates, you may be asked to provide your email address.

Your email address may be used to send technology news, tutorials, website updates and information about new digital tools.

We do not recommend entering another person's email address without their permission.

You may unsubscribe from newsletter or marketing communications where an unsubscribe option is provided.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TechHub Ghana may use cookies, local storage or similar browser technologies where required for website functionality, preferences, security or analytics.

Some website features may store information locally in your browser. Such information may include preferences or temporary application data.

Third-party services integrated into the website may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website features.

6. Third-Party Services

Some TechHub Ghana features depend on external technology providers. These may include website hosting, security, AI processing, search, fonts, icons, analytics and other technical services.

Cloudflare

TechHub Ghana uses Cloudflare services for certain website and AI infrastructure. This may include Cloudflare Workers and Workers AI.

Google Fonts

The website may load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may make a request to Google's servers when these fonts are loaded.

Font Awesome

The website may load icons from Font Awesome's content delivery infrastructure. Your browser may make a request to that service when the resources are loaded.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. TechHub Ghana is not responsible for privacy practices outside our control.

7. Digital Tools

TechHub Ghana provides tools such as GPA calculators, QR generators, password generators and other utilities.

Where a tool is designed to perform its work directly in your browser, the information entered into that tool may remain on your device and may not need to be sent to our servers.

Security reminder: Even when a tool is designed for local browser processing, avoid entering confidential information unless you understand exactly how that specific tool works.

Some tools or features may require server-side processing. Where this occurs, information necessary to provide the feature may be sent to the relevant service.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through TechHub Ghana.

However, no website, internet transmission, cloud service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For this reason, you should avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials or other highly sensitive information through ordinary website forms or AI features.

9. Children's Privacy

TechHub Ghana provides general technology education, news and digital tools.

We do not intentionally seek to collect sensitive personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has submitted personal information to TechHub Ghana that should not have been submitted, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and the type of information involved, you may have rights concerning personal information associated with you.

  • You can choose not to provide optional personal information.
  • You can stop using features that require information you do not want to provide.
  • You can unsubscribe from communications where an unsubscribe facility is available.
  • You can contact TechHub Ghana with questions or requests concerning information you have submitted to us.

Requests may be subject to verification and applicable legal requirements.

11. External Websites

TechHub Ghana may contain links to external websites, applications, services or resources.

External websites have their own privacy practices, terms and security policies.

TechHub Ghana is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external service before providing it with personal information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as TechHub Ghana introduces new features, services or technologies.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

13. Contact TechHub Ghana

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the way information is handled on TechHub Ghana, please contact us.
