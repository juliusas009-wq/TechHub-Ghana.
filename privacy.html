Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using TechHub Ghana.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TechHub Ghana. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit or use our website, including our technology news, tutorials, digital tools and AI Assistant.

By using TechHub Ghana, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate and improve TechHub Ghana.

Information you provide

  • Email addresses when you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Information you provide when contacting us.
  • Text that you voluntarily submit to the TechHub Ghana AI Assistant for processing.

Information collected automatically

Depending on the services and technologies used on the website, technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, IP address and website usage information may be processed by hosting, security, analytics or other technical service providers.

3. AI Assistant

TechHub Ghana provides an AI Assistant that can help with rewriting, summarizing, grammar correction and clearer writing.

Important: Do not submit passwords, financial information, confidential documents, personal identification numbers or other sensitive information to the AI Assistant.

When you submit text to the AI Assistant, the text is sent to our AI processing service so that the requested operation can be performed.

The AI Assistant is currently powered through Cloudflare Workers AI. TechHub Ghana does not intentionally request unnecessary personal information from users through the AI Assistant.

We do not claim ownership of the text you submit to the AI Assistant. You remain responsible for ensuring that you have the right to submit the content.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and operating TechHub Ghana services.
  • Processing requests made through our AI Assistant and other tools.
  • Sending newsletters or updates when you voluntarily subscribe.
  • Responding to questions and support requests.
  • Improving website functionality, security and user experience.
  • Detecting, preventing and addressing security or technical problems.

5. Newsletter and Email

If you subscribe to a TechHub Ghana newsletter, you may provide your email address so that we can send you technology news, tutorials, updates or other relevant information.

You may unsubscribe from marketing or newsletter communications where an unsubscribe option is provided.

We do not intend to sell your email address to third parties.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TechHub Ghana may use cookies or similar technologies when necessary to provide certain website functions, remember preferences, improve performance, or understand how the website is used.

Third-party services used by the website may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

7. Third-Party Services

TechHub Ghana may use third-party services to provide hosting, security, fonts, icons, AI processing, analytics, email services and other website functionality.

These third-party providers may process technical or other information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Examples of technologies currently used by TechHub Ghana include Cloudflare Workers AI, Google Fonts and Font Awesome.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through TechHub Ghana against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no website, internet connection or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide requested services, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes or protect the website.

Retention periods may also depend on the third-party service processing the information.

10. Children's Privacy

TechHub Ghana is intended to provide general technology, educational and digital information.

We do not intentionally request unnecessary personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate permission, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

11. Your Choices

Depending on the information involved, you may have choices regarding how your information is used.

  • You can choose not to submit personal information when it is not required.
  • You can choose not to use the AI Assistant.
  • You can unsubscribe from newsletters.
  • You can contact us with privacy-related questions or requests.

12. External Links

TechHub Ghana may contain links to external websites, applications or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external service you visit.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to TechHub Ghana, our services, technology or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed where appropriate.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the handling of information on TechHub Ghana, please contact us.

Email: info@techhubghana.com

You can also visit our Contact page for additional ways to reach TechHub Ghana.
