Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to TechHub Ghana. This page explains what information may be collected when you use our website and how that information may be used.

Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TechHub Ghana. We provide technology news, programming tutorials, artificial intelligence features and digital tools for students and other technology users.

By using TechHub Ghana, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, TechHub Ghana may receive limited information such as:

  • Information you voluntarily provide, such as an email address when using a newsletter subscription form.
  • Text that you voluntarily submit to our AI Assistant for processing.
  • Basic technical information that may be provided automatically by your browser, hosting provider or security services.
  • Website usage information that may be collected through analytics or similar technologies if such services are enabled.

3. AI Assistant Privacy

TechHub Ghana provides an AI Assistant that can help users rewrite, summarize, correct grammar and improve text.

Important: Do not submit passwords, banking details, private identification numbers or other highly sensitive information to the AI Assistant.

Text submitted to the AI Assistant is sent to our AI processing service so that the requested operation can be performed.

TechHub Ghana does not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the AI Assistant.

4. Newsletter Information

If you choose to subscribe to TechHub Ghana updates, you may be asked to provide your email address.

Your email address may be used to send technology news, tutorials, website updates and information about new tools.

You may unsubscribe from marketing or newsletter communications where an unsubscribe option is provided.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TechHub Ghana may use cookies or similar browser technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, preferences or analytics.

Third-party services used by the website may also use their own technologies according to their respective privacy policies.

6. Third-Party Services

Some TechHub Ghana features may depend on third-party services. These may include hosting, fonts, icons, AI processing, analytics or other technical services.

These third parties may process limited technical information or information required to provide their services.

Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Digital Tools

TechHub Ghana provides tools such as GPA calculators, QR generators, password generators and other utilities.

Whenever possible, these tools are designed to process information directly in your browser rather than sending it to a server.

However, users should always avoid entering confidential information into any online tool unless the tool specifically requires it and clearly explains how the information is handled.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through TechHub Ghana.

However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

TechHub Ghana is intended for general technology learning and information.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from children for purposes that require such collection.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information associated with you.

  • You can choose not to provide optional personal information.
  • You can stop using features that require information you do not want to provide.
  • You can unsubscribe from communications where an unsubscribe facility is available.
  • You can contact TechHub Ghana regarding questions about information submitted through the website.

11. External Websites

TechHub Ghana may contain links to external websites, services or resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of external websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external service you visit.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as TechHub Ghana introduces new features, services or technologies.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page.

13. Contact TechHub Ghana

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the way information is handled on TechHub Ghana, please contact us.
