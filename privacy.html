Privacy Policy

TechHub Ghana respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit our website, use our digital tools, submit information to our AI Assistant, or interact with other TechHub Ghana features.

Effective date: August 23, 2026

1. Who We Are

TechHub Ghana is a technology-focused website providing technology news, educational resources, programming tutorials, artificial intelligence features and free digital tools.

For information handled through this website, TechHub Ghana is the party responsible for determining the purposes for which information is processed, subject to the role of any third-party service provider involved in providing a particular feature.

For privacy questions, please use our Contact page .

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the TechHub Ghana website and the features provided through it, including technology content, tutorials, digital tools and the TechHub Ghana AI Assistant.

It also explains how information may be handled when our website communicates with third-party infrastructure used to operate particular features.

3. Information We May Process

The information involved depends on which TechHub Ghana features you use.

Information you provide

  • Text or other content that you voluntarily submit to the AI Assistant.
  • An email address if you voluntarily submit it through a newsletter or other contact feature.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting TechHub Ghana.

Information generated automatically

When you access a website, technical information may be processed by hosting, security, network and infrastructure providers. Depending on the service, this may include information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request information, approximate location derived from an IP address, timestamps and security-related information.

TechHub Ghana does not intentionally ask visitors to provide unnecessary personal information merely to browse ordinary website content.

4. Why Information May Be Processed

Information may be processed for purposes such as:

  • Providing and operating TechHub Ghana's website and features.
  • Processing requests submitted to the AI Assistant.
  • Responding to messages and support requests.
  • Providing newsletter or update communications where you have chosen to receive them.
  • Maintaining website security, preventing abuse and diagnosing technical problems.
  • Improving the reliability and functionality of the website.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations where required.

5. TechHub Ghana AI Assistant

TechHub Ghana provides an AI Assistant through a Cloudflare Worker. When you submit a request to the AI Assistant, the content of your request is sent from the website to our AI Worker so that the requested response can be generated.

Important: Please do not submit passwords, payment-card information, banking credentials, government identification numbers, authentication codes, confidential business information or other highly sensitive information to the AI Assistant.

Cloudflare Workers AI

Our AI Worker uses Cloudflare Workers AI to run an AI model and generate responses. Cloudflare describes prompts, outputs and other relevant AI content as Customer Content. Cloudflare states that it does not use Workers AI Customer Content to train the AI models available through Workers AI or improve Cloudflare or third-party services unless it receives explicit consent.

Cloudflare also states that Workers AI Customer Content may be stored if a customer separately uses a Cloudflare storage service such as R2, KV, Durable Objects or Vectorize together with Workers AI.

TechHub Ghana's AI Worker is intended to process a request and return the generated result. We do not intentionally ask users to provide sensitive personal information through the AI Assistant.

For additional information about Cloudflare's handling of information, please review Cloudflare's own Privacy Policy .

6. AI Search and Search Features

TechHub Ghana may provide AI-powered search or search-related features. When such a feature is used, the search query or other information necessary to perform the search may be transmitted to the service that processes that request.

Search requests should therefore not contain passwords, financial credentials, private identification numbers or other sensitive information that is not necessary to perform the search.

The specific processing of an AI Search request depends on the service and configuration used by TechHub Ghana at the time of the request.

7. Digital Tools

TechHub Ghana provides tools such as the GPA Calculator, QR Generator, Password Generator and other browser-based utilities.

Where a tool operates entirely within your browser, information entered into that tool may remain on your device and may not be transmitted to TechHub Ghana.

However, this should not be interpreted as a guarantee for every tool or future feature. Users should review the information displayed by a feature and avoid entering confidential information unless it is necessary.

Security reminder: Never use the Password Generator or other online tools as a place to store passwords, recovery codes, banking credentials or other secrets.

8. Newsletter and Email Information

TechHub Ghana may provide a newsletter or update subscription feature. If an email address is submitted through a functioning subscription service, that address may be processed for the purpose of delivering the requested communications.

We will not intentionally request information that is unnecessary for newsletter delivery.

If you no longer want to receive newsletter communications, you may unsubscribe using the unsubscribe mechanism provided with the communication, where available.

Current website note: If the newsletter form on the website has not been connected to an email collection or mailing service, submitting the form does not by itself mean that TechHub Ghana has created a permanent newsletter database.

9. Cookies and Local Browser Storage

TechHub Ghana may use browser technologies such as cookies, local storage or similar mechanisms when they are required for a particular feature.

Some website functionality may also store preferences or temporary information in your browser rather than on our servers.

Third-party services embedded in or loaded by the website may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their respective policies.

You can control or remove cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain browser technologies may cause some website features to operate differently.

11. Sharing of Information

TechHub Ghana does not intend to sell your personal information.

Information may be disclosed to service providers when disclosure is necessary to operate a requested feature, maintain the website, provide infrastructure, protect security or comply with a lawful requirement.

For example, information submitted to the AI Assistant must be transmitted to the Cloudflare infrastructure used to process the AI request.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security or property of TechHub Ghana, our users or others, or where disclosure is required by applicable law.

12. Data Retention

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

The retention period depends on the type of information, why it was collected, whether it is needed to provide a service, security requirements and applicable legal obligations.

Information processed by independent third-party providers may be retained according to those providers' own policies and contractual or legal requirements.

TechHub Ghana does not claim that every AI request is permanently stored by TechHub Ghana itself. The handling of AI request data also depends on the infrastructure and configuration used to process that request.

13. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information handled through TechHub Ghana.

These measures may include using HTTPS, limiting unnecessary data collection, protecting server-side credentials and using established infrastructure providers.

No internet transmission, website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should therefore avoid submitting information that they would not want exposed if a security incident occurred.

14. International Processing

Some technology and infrastructure providers used by TechHub Ghana may process information in countries outside Ghana.

This can occur because cloud, hosting, AI, content-delivery and other internet services may operate infrastructure across multiple locations.

Where applicable, such processing is subject to the provider's contractual, security and privacy arrangements and applicable law.

15. Children's Privacy

TechHub Ghana provides educational and technology content that may be useful to students and younger users.

We do not intentionally request unnecessary personal information from children.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information unnecessarily, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

16. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information processed about you.

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), data subjects have rights that can include rights relating to being informed about processing, access to personal information, and objection to certain processing.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable legal requirements, you may ask TechHub Ghana to:

  • Explain what personal information we process about you.
  • Provide access to personal information where applicable.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Consider an objection to processing where the law provides such a right.
  • Stop sending optional communications where an unsubscribe mechanism is available.

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions, verification requirements and other applicable limitations.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact TechHub Ghana through our Contact page .

17. External Websites and Links

TechHub Ghana may link to external websites, applications, articles, services or resources.

Once you leave TechHub Ghana and access an external service, that service's own privacy policy and terms may apply.

TechHub Ghana is not responsible for the privacy, security or content practices of independent external websites.

18. Ghana Data Protection

TechHub Ghana aims to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable Ghanaian data protection requirements.

Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) establishes principles concerning matters including accountability, lawfulness, purpose specification, openness, data security and data-subject participation.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy limits any mandatory rights or protections available to you under applicable law.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

TechHub Ghana may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, technology or legal obligations change.

When we make material changes, we will publish the updated version on this page and update the effective date shown above.

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, want to make a privacy request, or believe that information has been handled improperly, please contact TechHub Ghana.
